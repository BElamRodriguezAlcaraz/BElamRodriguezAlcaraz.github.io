About Research Teaching

I am an economist at Banco de México. My research interests include macroeconomics, labor markets, and monetary policy. I work with microdata from different sources and countries.

I hold an M.A. in Economics from El Colegio de México and a B.A. in Economics from UNAM.

In Mexico’s Labor Market through the Lens of the Beveridge Curve, my coauthors and I use data on formal-sector vacancies to study the relationship between job openings and unemployment in Mexico and its regions.

In joint work with Diana MacDonald, I study skill mismatch in the Mexican labor market and how differences between labor supply and demand vary across regions and industries.
